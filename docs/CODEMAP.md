# TIEFGANG A code map

Read the part you need; CLAUDE.md links here. Test scripts live outside the repo in /home/claude/pw (README lists each chunk's checks).

## src/core
display (integer scaler, bleed, safe area), lighting (room lamp: GLSL filter, palette SHADOW steps via LUT + Bayer dither; light.set, light.power), lockdown, haptics, audio (buses ui/machinery/bed/sensors/voices/music, mixer.duck), sfx (procedural foley; 3.6 drip, levels rebalanced (pw/loud36); SIG_RPM screw signatures steam4/steam3/diesel3/diesel4/turbine3; 3.1 page + book), store (clearStoreNow), settings (plotAssist, decodeAssist, tableAssist, torpFailures, prompts), tween, typed, font (one 6 px face), pixels, palette, tool-icons (pencil, eraser, ruler, dividers, zoom, boat, left, right, book, close).

## src/controls
Data-driven kit: key (icon, latch, styles iron/brass/paper), lever, crank, hold (+cover; lift(rate) runs it up by motor, pressed, rising), switch, button+cover, drag; createControl(spec); hitPolicy (44 pt targets); persistent controls restore from saves; meter, tape, odometer.

## src/obs (the only thing the UI reads)
- index.ts: obs store + send(command). ScopeObs (exposure 0..1.5) / Sight (cls, aob, degPerM, haze, marks; at sea also low, smoke, wave), ScopeMark event (bearing, range, aob, cls -> chart slips), drill obs/results (ScopeResult.ranged.set = class on the card), HydroCall/HydroMark, RadioObs, Commands (scopeSet, scopeMark {range, mast, cls, aob}, course, fix, askBearings, radioTune, crashDive...).
- ships.ts (3.1): the recognition manual. ShipClassKey (12: coaster collier tramp freighter ore tanker reefer liner | trawler corvette sloop destroyer), SHIP_CLASSES (no, name, warship, length, mast (all different), draught, tons, knots, maxKn, screws, look), MANUAL_ORDER, MERCHANT_KEYS, NEUTRALS (alvenda kestria norvale solmara), MARKS_RANGE, SCOPE_FOV/SURFACE.
- torpedo.ts (3.4): TORPEDOES (S steam 30/40/44 kn, E electric 30 kn), TorpSet, RUN (tube 30 m forward, reach 12, turn radius 95, arm 250), turnOut, TubeObs/TubesObs (obs.tubes every tick), eraOf, BOW_TUBES. Commands tubeSet/tubeFlood/tubeDoor (tube | tubes[] | 'all'; hand), tubeValve, reload/reloadCancel/reloadHands (3.5), gyroSet, fire {tubes, spread}, torpFailures; event torpedo launch; hull sounds launch, detonation(Far), clang, breakup. Sight.settle/trim; scope.wakes/blasts.
- attack.ts (3.3; 3.4: gyroFor iterates the real path, tube offset and turn): the attack table's arithmetic: TORPEDO {kn 40, range 7000}, Track (settings as laid at t0 from where the boat was), trackFrom, keep (position keeping from the reckoning), gyroFor (straight-runner intercept: gyro, run, time, track angle), Sources (mark|marks|hand|none, knSigma/aobSigma) + sigmas, band (corners of one standard error, kept to now; AOB ages 0.04 deg/s; half = angle her length covers), twoMarks (speed and course from two MARKs, sigma from the baseline), markedAt.
- bridge.ts: BridgeObs (view, sights with opaque keys; Outline = ShipClassKey; marks), sense.ts (Signature, sigKey, BLIND, SoundObs, LookoutReport, Signal, NavObs, EnvObs), grid.ts (gridOf/Name/Rect/Centre, KR 55 5 = centre), cipher.ts (disc cipher, key sheet, MORSE), boat.ts, power.ts, TIME_SCALES.

## src/sim (truth; only main.ts and src/debug may import it)
- engage.ts (3.6): the engagement (DrillKind 'attack'): ENGAGE_WORLD (fixed convoy WN 37: hulls by slot, escortSlots, zigzag -1 = straight), ENGAGE_PREROLL 360 s of world before the start, EngageState (brief/attack/over; wall = real seconds, firstSunkWall), ends on breakup heard (+14 s), out of reach (8 km, after 900 s; waits for a settling ship) or 4200 s; AttackResult. Sim.engagement(seed); drillNew 'attack' rebuilds the world in place (the UI restarts by reload instead).
- core.ts: Sim (fixed 10 Hz, commands on tick boundaries, effects + schedule, time compression, snapshot/load, lerped present). SAVE_VERSION 15, v4-v14 migrate (3.2 adds detect + rng.spot; 3.4 torp + rng.torp). stepTorp before stepSense (running fish join the sound field, own: true; the operator ignores them). Each tick: lens from the drill's hoist state -> sense.scopeWorld, detect.stepDetect. helm(), crashDive(), bombs().
- torpedo.ts (3.4): TorpWorld (SimState.torp, save v12, rng.torp; 3.5 v13: racks, reload): tubes (flood/door/set/drain jobs; hand variants: valve(), door/set with hand = no report, batched bow-room reports, wear), fire (fan, FAN_GAP 1.6 s), Fish runs (reach, turn, circle runners; depth keeper), hit test against every ship (draught, contact/magnetic, arming, glancing duds, prematures, own boat), FAILURES design table per era x conditions (latK, seaK, glanceK, wear) via rollFault, strike/strength (ShipState.hit: flood vs strength, stop, founder, sink), alarmed/threat (convoy turns, escorts hunt), wakes (steam tracks seen -> evade), sounds delayed by range/1500, sound room calls, log (truth outcomes for tests/debrief), scopeTorps, runPath (test geometry). 3.5: RELOAD_STEPS (fixed s or man-s), GANG 6, HAND_EXP 0.85, reloadSeconds(hands), RACKS {S 4, E 2}, reloads.order/cancel/hands, stepReload (tube busy 'reload'), ReloadObs in obs.tubes.reload.
- enemy-sense.ts (4.1): radiated(b, tw) (machinery + crew + bow-room work + launch, NOISES), kitAt(date) (asdic range, beam depression, radar), margins() per escort (asdic: 40log, aspect, sea, speed, astern, depression, 2x layer capped 26; ears: noise vs sea/self/convoy masking, layer; radar), stepHunt once a second (sweep coverage: search arc / hunt arc / on contact; echoWorth by doppler and HE; evidence FIRM 2, ebbs; contact -> convoy alarm, s.hunt on the datum, escortClosing call; FIRST OFFICER 'we are loud'); state in DetectState.esc/noise/contacts; rng.hunt (save v15). Silent running: BoatState.silent (boat.silent(); pumps gated unless sinking 8 m past, power quiet: fans off, SLOW -> one shaft 1.5 kn), reloads stall 'silent'.
- detect.ts (3.2): visibility(w, b, {lens, up}) (scope feather x speed x calm sea, broach/surfaced profile, x light), the exposure meter e (rise v/36, drain e/60, max 1.5), once a second each ship within min(5 km, vis) rolls ((e-0.2)+)^2 x reach x eye (escort 1.6, merchant 0.5) / 20 on rng.spot; sighted: group.alarm (turn away, full speed, 20 min), an escort's hunt (run to the spot, circle, 15 min), SCREWS SPEEDING UP if heard; first officer warnings.
- fleet.ts additions (3.4): ShipState.evade/hit (HitState)/sunk; stepShip: hurt ships hold course at hit.maxKn, evaders comb; group cap ignores hurt ships.
- world.ts (true world, 50 km BOX, landmarks, newWorld plans a convoy through the centre), fleet.ts (3.2: GroupState.alarm, ShipState.hunt; HULLS = one per ShipClassKey; convoys/escorts/independents; 3.1 variant() picks the finer type from a side Rng (collier/tramp for coaster slots, ore/reefer/liner for freighter slots, trawler for some slow-convoy corvettes), independents 40% neutral (ShipState.flag)), weather, sky, ocean, air.
- sense.ts: navigator (DR, fixes), scopeWorld(w, lens) (3.2: ships as the low lens sees them, hull down from its eye, kept for drill.present to merge; scopeSeen()), bridge() sights (marks for neutrals inside the hull range by day, 3.5 km at night), lookouts, hydrophone SNR, signals, judged weather. operator.ts: hydrophone operator (calls, classify incl. diesel4 = motor ship, op.ask). radio.ts: hourly broadcast. drill.ts: scope drill (merchant from MERCHANT_KEYS, 1 in 4 neutral from a side Rng, speed capped at 10 kn). boat.ts, power.ts, dive.ts, airdrill.ts.
